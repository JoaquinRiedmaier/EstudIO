# Proposed Study‑Tool Functionalities

## 1. Adaptive Quiz Engine
- Generates personalized quizzes based on learner proficiency.
- Implements spaced‑repetition scheduling to reinforce mastery.

## 2. Interactive Lesson Modules
- Embeds videos, simulations, and drag‑and‑drop activities.
- Supports real‑time feedback and progress tracking.

## 3. AI Homework Helper
- Conversational agent that explains concepts and solves problems step‑by‑step.
- Offers hints and references to learning resources.

## 4. Collaborative Study Rooms
- Real‑time shared whiteboard, document editor, and voice chat.
- Enables group brainstorming, peer review, and joint problem solving.

## 5. Curriculum Planner & Syllabus Mapper
- Allows instructors to align courses with standards and schedule topics.
- Exports syllabi in multiple formats (PDF, markdown, LMS import).

## 6. Progress Dashboard
- Visualizes student performance, mastery levels, and time‑on‑task.
- Provides analytics for teachers to identify at‑risk learners.

## 7. LMS Integration (Moodle/Canvas APIs)
- Syncs enrollments, grades, and course content.
- Supports two‑way data flow for seamless classroom management.

## 8. Resource Library with Search
- Central repository of textbooks, lecture notes, videos, and external links.
- Keyword and faceted search for quick resource discovery.

## 9. Automated Grading & Feedback
- Evaluates coding assignments, essays, and multiple‑choice questions.
- Returns rubric‑based feedback and mastery scores automatically.

## 10. Peer Review Workflow
- Structured peer‑review cycles with calibrated rubrics.
- Includes discussion threads for feedback exchange.

## 11. Mobile Companion App
- Offers offline access to lessons, quizzes, and progress data.
- Push notifications for reminders and new content.

## 12. Learning Analytics & Insights
- Aggregates usage metrics to reveal curriculum effectiveness and at‑risk patterns.
- Provides actionable insights for educators and administrators.
